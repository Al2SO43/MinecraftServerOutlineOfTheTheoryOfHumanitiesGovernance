
## 完整术语表 / Glossary of Key Terms

本文涉及原创理论概念,Minecraft中文社区特定术语及跨文化易混淆词汇,在此统一界定,以便检索与引用.

This article involves original theoretical concepts, specific terms from the Chinese Minecraft community, and cross-cultural terms that are easily confused, which are uniformly defined here for the purposes of retrieval and citation.

### A. 原创理论框架 / Original Theoretical Framework

| 中文术语          | English Term                                  | 定义 / Definition                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ------------- | --------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **多力量中心制衡格局** | **Polycentric Checks-and-Balances Framework** | 管理者、玩家群体（及其自然团体化集团）与游戏实际内容三方力量在服务器中具象化并相互制衡，同时受外部非直接力量调节的复杂多元结构。<br>The complex multi-actor structure in which administrators, player groups (and their naturally formed collectives), and actual game content are concretized and mutually check and balance one another, while being continuously adjusted by indirect external forces. |
| **管玩矛盾**      | **Admin-Player Structural Contradiction**     | 管理者维护服务器长期发展与玩家追求个体发展之间的固有、不可消除的结构性冲突，贯穿服务器生命周期。<br>The inherent and irremovable structural conflict between administrators' pursuit of long-term server development and players' pursuit of individual advancement, running through the entire server lifecycle.                                                                         |
| **本质淡化**      | **Essential Dilution**                        | 事物在发展过程中逐渐剥离其初心、本质或功能定位的系统性偏移，可为被动亦可为主动。<br>The systematic drift by which a thing gradually sheds its original purpose, essential nature, or functional positioning as it develops, which may occur either passively or actively.                                                                                                         |
| **逆本质淡化**     | **Reverse Essential Dilution**                | 发现问题后，通过制度机制等手段使偏离初心的事物恢复其原本性质与功能的治理行为。<br>A governance action that, after identifying deviation, uses institutional mechanisms to restore a thing to its original nature and function.                                                                                                                                                   |
| **接受本质淡化**    | **Accepting Essential Dilution**              | 对偏离初心但不对服务器发展造成恶性影响的结果予以保留，并将其纳入新的治理框架。<br>The decision to retain a result that has deviated from its original purpose but does not cause malignant harm to server development, integrating it into a new governance framework.                                                                                                           |
| **周期性与相似性定理** | **Theorems of Periodicity and Similarity**    | 服务器治理的两大普遍规律：玩家活跃度与问题爆发呈周期性变化；不同周目及同类服务器之间的发展轨迹具有高度相似性。<br>Two universal laws of server governance: player activity and problem outbreaks follow periodic patterns; developmental trajectories across different cycles and similar servers exhibit high similarity.                                                                       |
| **五个基本问题**    | **Five Fundamental Problems**                 | 服务器治理中最普遍存在的五类问题：玩家团体化、经济危机、代差、玩法僵化、近距离资源枯竭。<br>The five most universal problems in server governance: player collectivization, economic crisis, generational gap, gameplay stagnation, and proximate resource exhaustion.                                                                                                                |
| **广义代差**      | **Generalized Generational Gap**              | 管理层内部（认知、技术、道德等）及游戏内容设计层面存在的水平或垂直差异。<br>Horizontal or vertical disparities existing within the administrative layer (cognition, technology, ethics, etc.) or in game content design.                                                                                                                                                      |
| **狭义代差**      | **Narrow-Sense Generational Gap**             | 玩家之间因进服时间、游戏理解或技术水平差异自然形成的分层，尤以新老玩家代差为典型。<br>Natural stratification among players caused by differences in join time, game comprehension, or technical skill, most typically embodied in the veteran-newcomer gap.                                                                                                                        |
| **近距离资源枯竭**   | **Proximate Resource Exhaustion**             | 在单世界、固定出生点的生存/战争服务器中，出生点周围资源被老玩家开发殆尽，导致新玩家发展受阻的现象。<br>The phenomenon in single-world, fixed-spawn survival/war servers where resources around spawn points are depleted by veteran players, hindering newcomers' development.                                                                                                             |
| **等级对立**      | **Hierarchical Opposition**                   | 因身份、资源、权力或信息差异导致的高低等级玩家之间的利益冲突与制度性不公。<br>Conflict and institutional injustice between higher- and lower-ranked players arising from differences in status, resources, power, or information.                                                                                                                                              |
| **真等级对立**     | **True Hierarchical Opposition**              | 基于外部资源（如金钱、管理特权）或封闭上升通道造成的、不可通过游戏内努力逆转的等级不公。<br>Rank injustice based on external resources (e.g., money, administrative privileges) or closed upward mobility channels, which cannot be reversed through in-game effort.                                                                                                                  |
| **伪等级对立**     | **Pseudo-Hierarchical Opposition**            | 基于游戏内实际努力（如时间投入、技术积累）形成的差异，新玩家可通过合理途径达到同等水平的"误判型等级差异"。<br>A misperceived rank difference based on actual in-game effort (e.g., time invested, technical accumulation), which newcomers can overcome through reasonable means to reach equivalent levels.                                                                                  |
| **玩家团体化**     | **Player Collectivization**                   | 玩家从个体状态逐步结成利益共同体（玩家团体）的必然社会过程，是玩家群体化的最直接表现形式。<br>The inevitable social process by which individual players gradually coalesce into interest-based communities (player groups), representing the most direct manifestation of player collectivization.                                                                                     |
| **倒挂格局**      | **Inverted Power Structure**                  | 玩家团体实力过大，形成足以架空管理层的非对称力量格局，破坏多力量中心制衡。<br>An asymmetric power configuration in which player groups grow strong enough to hollow out administrative authority, thereby destroying the polycentric checks-and-balances framework.                                                                                                            |
### B. 服务器治理机制 / Governance Mechanisms

| 中文术语        | English Term                        | 定义 / Definition                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ----------- | ----------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **民主议事**    | **Democratic Deliberation**         | 在服务器中建立的、允许玩家群体通过提议与讨论参与服务器决策的制度体系，旨在将对抗性矛盾转化为程序性博弈。<br>An institutional system established within a server that allows player groups to participate in server decision-making through proposals and discussions, aiming to transform antagonistic contradictions into procedural contestation. |
| **人文规则**    | **Humanistic Rules**                | 区别于游戏机制规则的、基于公序良俗与服务器治理需要而制定的玩家守则、管理员守则及惩戒申诉体系。<br>Player codes, administrator codes, and disciplinary/appeal systems formulated on the basis of public order, good customs, and governance needs, distinct from mechanical game rules.                                                         |
| **管理层体制**   | **Administrative System**           | 关于管理者权力来源、组织形式、选拔任免、监督惩戒的一整套制度安排。<br>The complete set of institutional arrangements concerning the source of administrative power, organizational forms, selection and removal, and supervision and discipline.                                                                                 |
| **大陆法系式演化** | **Civil-Law-Style Rule Evolution**  | 以成文规则为中心，通过发现问题—设立新规范—再适用的路径完善人文规则，判例仅作参考。<br>A rule evolution path centered on written statutes, improving humanistic rules through the cycle of identifying problems—establishing new norms—reapplying them, where precedents serve only as reference.                                        |
| **英美法系式演化** | **Common-Law-Style Rule Evolution** | 以实际判例为中心，通过遵循先例与裁判者解释创造新规则的路径完善人文规则。<br>A rule evolution path centered on actual precedents, improving humanistic rules through following prior cases and creating new rules via adjudicator interpretation.                                                                                    |
| **习惯法（社区）** | **Customary Law (Community-Based)** | 在社区舆论中被普遍认可为违规或不违规的、尚未被明文写入规则的行为规范。<br>Behavioral norms not yet codified into written rules but generally recognized as compliant or non-compliant within community public opinion.                                                                                                             |
| **善意违规**    | **Benevolent Violation**            | 出于维护秩序、正当防卫或其他正当事由而违反人文规则，但主观恶意极低的行为。<br>An act that violates humanistic rules out of motives such as maintaining order, legitimate self-defense, or other justifiable reasons, but with extremely low subjective malice.                                                                       |
| **不告不理**    | **Non-Action Without Complaint**    | 管理者仅在当事方主动上告或公开说明后才介入调解，否则不予主动干预的原则。<br>The principle that administrators intervene only after the concerned parties actively file a complaint or make a public statement, refraining from proactive intervention otherwise.                                                                    |
| **同态复仇**    | **Retaliatory Justice**             | 允许受害方以同等方式回击侵害方的规则设定，需严格限定适用范围以防防卫过当。<br>A rule setting that allows the injured party to retaliate against the offender in an equivalent manner, requiring strict scope limitations to prevent excessive defense.                                                                               |

### C. 游戏经济学 / In-Game Economics

| 中文术语          | English Term                      | 定义 / Definition                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ------------- | --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **产能-稀有度原理**  | **Productivity-Rarity Principle** | 游戏物品的价值与其可量产程度负相关：当产能超过阈值，稀有度与价值将急剧下降。<br>The principle that the value of in-game items is negatively correlated with their mass-producibility: once productivity exceeds a threshold, rarity and value drop sharply.                |
| **价值定律（游戏内）** | **Law of Value (In-Game)**        | 游戏物品的价值归根结底取决于玩家获取它所付出的时间成本，并受主观需求与稀有度调节。<br>The law stating that the value of in-game items is ultimately determined by the time cost players expend to obtain them, adjusted by subjective demand and rarity.                      |
| **时间成本**      | **Time Cost**                     | 玩家为获取某一游戏物品或达成某一游戏目标而在服务器中付出的时间支出，是游戏经济学中最核心的价值度量。<br>The expenditure of time that players invest in a server to obtain a particular in-game item or achieve a specific goal, serving as the core value metric in in-game economics. |
| **简单经济学问题**   | **Simple Economics Problem**      | 仅涉及玩家个体与游戏系统之间交互的经济问题，不存在玩家间市场流通。<br>Economic problems involving only the interaction between an individual player and the game system, without market circulation among players.                                                    |
| **复杂经济学问题**   | **Complex Economics Problem**     | 涉及玩家之间通过市场形式进行物品与货币流通、时间成本在自然人主体间转移的经济问题。<br>Economic problems involving the circulation of items and currency among players through market mechanisms and the transfer of time costs between natural persons.                       |
| **复合问题**      | **Composite Economics Problem**   | 同时包含简单部分（玩家-系统交互）与复杂部分（玩家间市场交易）的经济问题。<br>Economic problems that simultaneously contain a simple component (player-system interaction) and a complex component (inter-player market transactions).                                    |
| **管玩贸易**      | **Admin-Player Trade**            | 管理者以创造模式或系统商店身份直接参与市场交易的行为，具有时间成本蒸发风险。<br>The act of administrators directly participating in market transactions in creative mode or as system shops, carrying the risk of time-cost evaporation.                                   |
| **固定汇率制**     | **Fixed Exchange Rate System**    | 由管理层人为设定物品与货币之间固定兑换比率的经济机制，易导致玩法趋同与货币超发。<br>An economic mechanism in which the administration artificially fixes the exchange ratio between items and currency, easily leading to gameplay homogenization and currency oversupply.   |
| **浮动汇率制**     | **Floating Exchange Rate System** | 由管理层根据市场情况动态调整兑换比率的经济机制，实践成本高昂。<br>An economic mechanism in which the administration dynamically adjusts exchange ratios according to market conditions, entailing high implementation costs.                                        |
| **经济周期**      | **Economic Cycle**                | 服务器经济沿繁荣、衰退、萧条、复苏四阶段交替运行的规律性过程。<br>The regular process in which a server's economy alternately runs through the four stages of prosperity, recession, depression, and recovery.                                                      |

### D. 社区特定术语 / Community-Specific Terms

| 中文术语      | English Term                                  | 定义 / Definition                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| --------- | --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **圈钱服**   | **Quanqian Server** (cash-grab server)        | 以短期盈利为核心目的，通过付费特权、虚假宣传等手段榨取玩家后删档跑路的劣质服务器。<br>A low-quality server focused on short-term profit via paid privileges and false advertising, often shutting down after extracting value from players.            |
| **神权服**   | **Shenquan Server** (theocratic-admin server) | 管理员拥有不受制约的绝对权力，以特权化、等级化方式治理的服务器。<br>A server in which administrators hold unchecked absolute power and govern in a privileged, hierarchical manner.                                                           |
| **快餐服**   | **Fast-Food Server**                          | 生命周期极短、追求快速盈利、缺乏长期运营规划的服务器类型。<br>A server type characterized by an extremely short lifecycle, pursuit of quick profit, and lack of long-term operational planning.                                            |
| **公益服**   | **Non-Profit Server**                         | 由管理者自费或仅靠自愿捐赠维持，不以盈利为主要目的的服务器。<br>A server maintained by administrators at their own expense or through voluntary donations, not operated primarily for profit.                                               |
| **服主**    | **Server Owner**                              | 服务器的最高所有者、最高决策者及象征性领袖，区别于一般管理员。<br>The supreme owner, highest decision-maker, and symbolic leader of a server, distinct from ordinary administrators.                                                         |
| **周目**    | **Server Cycle** (Zhoumu)                     | 服务器从开服到存档重置的完整运营周期。<br>A full operational cycle from a server's launch to its world reset.                                                                                                                    |
| **重开周目**  | **Cycle Reset**                               | 服务器主动重置存档，使所有玩家回到同一起跑线，开启新一轮运营周期。<br>The active reset of a server's world archive, returning all players to the same starting line to begin a new operational cycle.                                          |
| **生电**    | **Redstone Engineering / Technical Survival** | 利用游戏红石机制建造自动化生产设备（如刷铁机、刷怪塔）的高阶玩法。<br>An advanced playstyle utilizing the game's redstone mechanics to build automated production facilities (e.g., iron farms, mob grinders).                                 |
| **看海**    | **Spectator Governance**                      | 管理员仅旁观玩家发展而不主动干预的治理状态，常见于无规则服务器。<br>A governance state in which administrators merely observe player development without active intervention, commonly seen in no-rules servers.                              |
| **面包人**   | **Bread-And-Butter Players**                  | 在大型建筑或生电项目中从事基础重复劳动的普通玩家，是社区对非技术核心成员的戏称。<br>Ordinary players engaged in basic repetitive labor in large-scale building or redstone-engineering projects, a community nickname for non-technical core members. |
| **矿透**    | **X-Ray / Ore Transparency**                  | 通过修改客户端或材质包透视地下矿物的行为，在多数服务器中被视为作弊。<br>The act of seeing underground ores by modifying the client or resource pack, regarded as cheating on most servers.                                                      |
| **六边形战士** | **Hexagonal Warrior** (all-rounder)           | 形容在技术、管理、沟通、建筑、红石、外交等各方面均无短板的复合型人才。<br>A term describing a versatile talent with no obvious weaknesses across technology, management, communication, building, redstone, and diplomacy.                       |
| **废权运动**  | **Abolish-Admin Movement**                    | 玩家群体中以极端方式要求废除所有管理者、实行完全自治的激进思潮。<br>A radical ideology within player groups demanding the abolition of all administrators and the implementation of complete self-governance.                                 |
| **存档**    | **World Save**                                | 服务器游戏世界的数据文件，包含地图、建筑、物品等全部游戏状态。<br>The data file of a server's game world, containing the complete game state including the map, buildings, and items.                                                        |
| **核心**    | **Server Core**                               | 服务器底层软件（如 Spigot、Paper、Forge 等），决定服务器性能与可扩展性。<br>The underlying server software (e.g., Spigot, Paper, Forge) that determines server performance and scalability.                                              |

### E. 方法论与引用语 / Methodology & Cultural References

| 中文术语            | English Term                                        | 定义 / Definition                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| --------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **从玩家中来，到玩家中去** | **From the Players, To the Players**                | 管理者深入玩家群体收集意见，经提炼后再以制度形式服务于玩家的工作方法。<br>A working method in which administrators immerse themselves among players to collect opinions, refine them, and then serve players through institutional forms.                  |
| **向死而生**        | **Facing Death to Embrace Life**                    | 正视服务器必然消亡的规律，将每一次衰败视为制度革新与螺旋上升的契机。<br>The attitude of confronting the inevitable demise of a server, treating each decline as an opportunity for institutional innovation and spiral ascent.                            |
| **螺旋式上升**       | **Spiral Ascent**                                   | 服务器在周期性循环中并非原地转圈，而是通过经验积累实现治理水平的阶段性超越。<br>The process by which a server, through periodic cycles, does not merely spin in place but achieves stage-by-stage transcendence in governance through accumulated experience. |
| **具体问题具体分析**    | **Concrete Analysis of Concrete Conditions**        | 反对教条主义与机械照搬，强调一切治理方案必须基于服务器的实际情境。<br>The methodological principle opposing dogmatism and mechanical copying, emphasizing that all governance solutions must be based on the concrete conditions of a given server.      |
| **水能载舟，亦能覆舟**   | **Water Can Carry a Boat, But Can Also Capsize It** | 玩家群体是服务器生存的基础，也是足以终结服务器的终极力量。<br>The metaphor that the player group is both the foundation of a server's survival and the ultimate force capable of ending it.                                                          |
| **劣币驱逐良币**      | **Bad Money Drives Out Good**                       | 低质量服务器（圈钱服）通过不正当竞争驱逐高质量公益服务器的市场现象。<br>The market phenomenon in which low-quality servers (cash-grab servers) drive out high-quality non-profit servers through unfair competition.                                      |

| **创新窗口**<br>（扩展02）  | **Innovation Window**   | 在治理刚性划定的边界之内，通过制度创新、文化塑造与机制设计可调节治理具体形态的制度空间。<br>The institutional space within governance rigidity boundaries where governance forms can be adjusted through institutional innovation, cultural shaping, and mechanism design.                                    |
